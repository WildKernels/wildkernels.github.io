03 three columns — with one-line explanations

Prebuilt GKI kernels · Android 12–16

KernelSU
gives you
Root

Kernel-level root that survives Play Integrity checks better than a userspace patch.

SUSFS
gives you
Hiding

Hides the root, the mounts and the modified files from apps that look for them.

BBG
gives you
Protection

Blocks writes to the baseband and critical partitions, so a bad module can't brick you.

All three are patched into the kernel source and ship in every build. Pick your version, flash the zip, done.
